## The ecosystem

VORTEX is an ecosystem of four modular applications designed for television production and real-time communication.

### VORTEX Regia — the control room

VORTEX Regia combines switching, audio, tally, replay, graphics and rundown management and incorporates **NewsIntelligence**, an AI-powered news aggregator that gathers configured sources, evaluates stories and suggests the newscast rundown. The workflow runs from news gathering to air; final decisions remain with the newsroom and control room.

### VORTEX Intercom — the matrix

A software intercom in external beta testing, with TALK, LISTEN, party-line, IFB and support for up to 16 N-1 circuits on compatible interfaces. The control room manages the matrix and assigns positions through signed invitations.

![VORTEX Intercom](assets/prodotti/intercom.jpg)

[**→ vortex-intercom**](https://github.com/Solarys431/vortex-intercom) · [landing](https://solarys431.github.io/vortex-intercom/)

### VORTEX Meet — multilingual meetings

VORTEX Meet provides multilingual meetings with simultaneous translation across ten languages, translated captions, and AI-generated minutes and summaries at the end of the session.

![VORTEX Meet](assets/prodotti/meet.jpg)

[**→ vortex-communication-os**](https://github.com/Solarys431/vortex-communication-os) · [landing](https://solarys431.github.io/vortex-communication-os/)

### VORTEX AI Graphic Studio — graphics

VORTEX AI Graphic Studio controls broadcast graphics through voice commands, Gemini assistance, SPX-GC and CasparCG playout.

## The vision

The ecosystem's common principle is: **AI suggests, the newsroom and control room decide**. The critical on-air path is designed to remain independent from AI services; this architecture still requires field validation.

---

## Status

**Regia, Meet and AI Graphic Studio: functional prototypes. Intercom: external beta test through TestFlight.** The static site uses no cookies or tracking; product images use demonstration data.
